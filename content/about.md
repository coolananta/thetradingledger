## About this journal

<span class="pill pill-slate">Author: Anonymous</span>

The Trading Ledger is a personal trading journal, published under no name. Every entry is written the same day a session happens, using the actual fills, prices, and levels from that session — not a reconstructed or idealized version of it afterward.

The goal here isn't to sell a strategy or claim a win rate. It's to keep an honest, dated record: what the setup looked like, what was actually done about it, and — just as often — what should have been done instead. Readers looking for a polished track record should look elsewhere; readers looking for a real one are exactly who this is for.

{{< callout type="note" title="Not financial advice" >}}
Nothing published here is investment, financial, or trading advice, and none of it should be treated as a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Every post is a personal record of one trader's decisions, written after the fact. See the [Disclaimer](/disclaimer/) page for the full statement.
{{< /callout >}}
