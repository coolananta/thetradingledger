## What this site collects

The Trading Ledger collects only what's submitted voluntarily through the **Subscribe** and **Contact** forms on the [About](/about/) page:

- **Subscribe form:** your email address, used solely to send new posts from this journal.
- **Contact form:** your name, email address, and message, used solely to respond to your message.

This site does not use tracking cookies, third-party advertising pixels, or analytics that identify individual visitors beyond aggregate, anonymized traffic reporting via Google Analytics.

## How form data is handled

Form submissions are processed by Netlify Forms, the hosting provider for this site. Netlify stores submissions so they can be retrieved by the site owner; see [Netlify's own privacy policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/) for details on how they handle that data on their infrastructure.

## Comments

If you leave a comment on a post, it is handled by giscus, which stores comments as GitHub Discussions on the site's associated GitHub repository. Commenting requires a GitHub account and is subject to [GitHub's privacy policy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## Your choices

You can unsubscribe from journal emails at any time by replying to any email you receive, or by emailing [ganon298@gmail.com](mailto:ganon298@gmail.com) directly with an unsubscribe request. To request deletion of any data submitted through the contact form, email the same address.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes in how the site operates. Check back here for the current version.
